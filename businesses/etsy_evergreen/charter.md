## 4. Duration & stop-loss

- **Duration:** 60 days from launch.
- **Stop-loss (kill triggers — any one ends the experiment):**
  - Spend reaches 100% of total cap with revenue < $200.
  - Day 30 checkpoint: revenue < $250 AND no week-over-week growth.
  - Unit economics negative after 20 orders (can't price/cost its way out).
- **Kill procedure:** pause campaigns → snapshot P&L → archive lessons → TERMINATED.

## 5. Approval boundaries

| Action | Authority |
|--------|-----------|
| Spend within caps | Agents (auto) |
| Raise any cap | Human |
| New platform/account | Human |
| Refunds > $100 | Human (escalated) |
| Shut down | Human (on stop-loss trigger or recommendation) |

## 6. Learning capture

Win or lose, the experiment writes to agent memory: what worked, what failed,
supplier notes, pricing learnings. The next experiment starts smarter.
