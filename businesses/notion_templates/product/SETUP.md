# Setup — building and selling the pack

## Part 1: Build it in Notion (owner, one time, ~1 hour)

1. Create a free Notion workspace (or a fresh page tree in an
   existing one) named **Freelancer OS**.
2. Build the three databases from `templates/02`, `03`, `04`
   exactly as specified — property names matter, the formulas and
   rollups reference them.
3. Add the relations both ways (Clients ↔ Projects ↔ Invoices) and
   the rollups (Lifetime value, Invoiced, Paid).
4. Enter the `[Sample]` entries so every view shows something.
5. Build the **Freelancer HQ** page (`templates/01`) with the four
   linked views, filtered as specified.
6. Add the three page templates from `templates/05` (proposal,
   meeting notes, weekly planner) as pages under HQ.
7. Click through every view once. If a filter shows nothing, a
   property name is off — fix the name, not the filter.

## Part 2: Make it a product (owner, one time)

1. On the Freelancer OS root page: **Share → Publish → Share to
   web**, and enable **Allow duplicate as template**.
2. Copy the public link. That link IS the product: a buyer opens
   it, clicks Duplicate, and owns a copy.
3. Set a calendar reminder to re-check the link monthly (Notion
   occasionally resets share settings on plan changes).

## Part 3: Deliver it (per sale)

- **Gumroad:** create the product, and put the duplicate link in
  the post-purchase content (Gumroad shows it to buyers only).
  Gumroad handles tax/VAT remittance for digital goods in most
  regions — confirm the current terms at signup.
- **Etsy:** digital-download listing. Upload a one-page PDF whose
  only content is the duplicate link + the 3-step "how to duplicate"
  instructions below. Etsy does not remit VAT everywhere for
  digital goods — check your obligations before listing.

## The buyer's first five minutes (goes in the PDF / Gumroad content)

1. Open the template link.
2. Click **Duplicate** (top right) and pick your workspace.
3. Open **Clients**, **Projects**, **Invoices** and delete every
   entry marked `[Sample]`.
4. Rename the HQ page's quick-link buttons if your Notion plan
   shows them as unavailable — plain links work the same.
5. Add your first real client. You're live.
