## 1. The bet

- **Niche:** Digital planners & organizers on Etsy
- **Product line (12 listings):** ADHD daily/weekly planners (hyperlinked PDF),
  wedding budget + timeline spreadsheet suite, small-business content calendar kit,
  kids chore-chart bundle — all in the $8–15 sweet spot.
- **Why this, why now:** Oct 2026 market data — ADHD planners, wedding suites
  ($34.99, 1.4K reviews), and hyperlinked PDF planners are trending; printable
  planners avg $4.93 and digital planners $6.97 with 5.8M monthly views on the
  category. Etsy supplies organic search traffic (no audience needed to start).
  Zero COGS, ~88% contribution margin per sale. Lowest capital of the three
  niches evaluated; closest credible path to $1K in 60 days.

| Metric | Target | Measured by |

Unit economics per $12 sale: −$0.78 (6.5% txn) −$0.61 (3%+$0.25 processing)
−$0.02 (listing amortized) = **+$10.59 contribution**.

## 4. Duration & stop-loss

- **Duration:** 60 days from first listing live.
- **Kill triggers (any one ends it):**
  - Spend hits $91 with revenue < $200.
  - Day 30 checkpoint: revenue < $250 AND no week-over-week growth for 2 weeks.
  - 20 orders in and contribution margin negative (safety; not expected at 88%).
- **Kill procedure:** pause Etsy Ads → snapshot P&L → archive lessons to agent
  memory → business → TERMINATED.
