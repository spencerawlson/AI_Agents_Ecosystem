## Description

**Your whole freelance practice. One Notion workspace. No
spreadsheets.**

Freelancer OS is a five-template Notion system built for solo
freelancers who are tired of chasing invoices in one app, projects
in another, and clients in their inbox:

- **Freelancer HQ** — one home page that answers four questions:
  what do I do today, what's in flight, who owes me money, who do I
  chase?
- **Client CRM** — every client, rate, and follow-up date, with a
  pipeline view so leads never quietly die.
- **Project Tracker** — projects linked to clients and invoices,
  with live balances: fee, invoiced, paid, still owed.
- **Invoice & Income Tracker** *(Complete)* — due-date math built
  in. Overdue invoices flag themselves 🔴; income-by-month and
  tax-year views do the arithmetic for you.
- **Proposals & Meetings** *(Complete)* — a proposal template that
  closes, meeting notes that turn into tasks, and a Monday weekly
  planner.

**How it works:** buy, open the link, click Duplicate — the whole
system lands in your Notion in under a minute. Sample entries show
you exactly how it works; delete them and add your first client.

**You'll get:** the duplicable template link + a one-page quick
start. Works with a free Notion account.

**Not included:** this is a template, not software or coaching. No
Notion account is provided. Formulas and views only use standard
Notion features.
